N 25.27° · Old Port · Est. 2019

Specialty coffee · Old Port District

Poured slowly. Shared generously.

Qahwa House roasts single-origin beans in small batches and serves them two ways: the cardamom-and-saffron qahwa of our grandmothers, and the precise pour-overs of the modern bar. Pull up a cushion.

02 — Origins

Three farms, one roaster.

We buy directly from growers we have visited, pay well above the commodity price, and roast every Tuesday and Friday in a five-kilo drum behind the counter.

03 — The House

A majlis for slow mornings.

Low brass tables, woven cushions and a long walnut bar where you can watch every cup being made. There is no Wi-Fi password on the wall — ask the barista, and stay as long as you like.

How we pour qahwa

  1. 1

    We pour with the left hand and offer the cup with the right.

  2. 2

    Your finjan is filled only a third of the way, so every sip is hot.

  3. 3

    When you have had enough, give the cup a gentle shake from side to side.
